Introducing Goomapay

One screen. Your whole circle.

Goomapay is a savings circle for the Stellar network. Create a circle with people you trust, agree on how much and how often to contribute, and let the rotation handle the rest.

Every contribution and every payout moves in USDC, settles on Stellar in seconds, and is recorded permanently on-chain. You always know who has paid, who's next, and exactly how much is in the pot.

Your funds sit in your own wallet between cycles — not on a platform, not in an admin's bank account. Even if Goomapay were offline, the money would still be yours.

Why Goomapay Exists

Everything a savings circle needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Rotational savings has always run on trust between people. Goomapay keeps that spirit and removes the risk — the missed payout, the disappearing treasurer, the paperwork nobody can check.

◔

Save With People, Not a Platform

Circles are formed by people you choose — family, coworkers, traders. Goomapay never centralizes the pot; it moves wallet to wallet on Stellar.

↻

Your Turn, Automatically

The rotation is enforced on-chain. When a cycle closes, the next member is paid automatically — no chasing, no treasurer who goes quiet.

◈

Your Money Stays Yours

Funds sit in your own Stellar wallet between cycles, not on Goomapay's servers. No platform can freeze or withhold your contribution.

⚡

Payouts Under 5 Seconds

Stellar settles the payout straight to the recipient's wallet almost instantly — not the 2–3 days a bank transfer takes.

⇄

Move In and Out Freely

On-ramp local currency into USDC to join a circle. Off-ramp your payout back to cash the moment you collect it.

✓

Verify Every Contribution

Every payment in and out of the circle is a transaction on the Stellar ledger. Check it yourself, anytime, without asking anyone.

Simple. Fast. Secure.

How a circle runs on Goomapay

Four steps, repeating every cycle, for as long as your circle chooses to run.

1

Start or Join a Circle

Pick your group, set the contribution amount, and choose how often the cycle repeats.

2

Contribute in USDC

Each member sends their share on schedule, directly from their own Stellar wallet.

3

The Rotation Turns

When the cycle closes, Goomapay's on-chain logic pays the member whose turn it is — instantly.

4

Collect and Spend

Your payout lands in USDC. Off-ramp it to cash, or spend it straight from your wallet.

Safety · Speed · Security

Money that moves fast, and never leaves your hands

Every design decision in Goomapay starts from one question: does this keep the member in control of their own money?

Safety

Contributions and payouts move directly between member wallets on Stellar. Goomapay never pools your money into a company account — there is nothing for a platform to lose or freeze.

Speed

Stellar settles transactions in seconds for a fraction of a cent — so a payout reaches your wallet before you'd finish checking your balance on a legacy bank app.

Security

Every contribution, rotation, and payout is a cryptographically signed transaction on the Stellar ledger — a permanent, public record no single party can quietly rewrite.
